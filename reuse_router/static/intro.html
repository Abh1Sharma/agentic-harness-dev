Gap 1 · Findability

Teams can't tell that a certified asset already does what they need, so they rebuild it, and it has to be certified again.

Gap 2 · Proof

An approval is a status in a system. Other systems can't check it, and it doesn't notice when the code changes.

The two gaps are our working hypothesis, to be checked with the marketplace's users.

The idea

Two additions, one principle.

AI reads · rules decide · people approve · evidence is automatic

Uses Jev

Discover

Describe a need in plain English. Jev reads it; plain code decides whether to reuse, extend or build, and whether the asset is certified for your data.

No AI, by design

SafeAI Passport

Every certified asset carries a signed certificate. Change one field and it fails verification. Change the code and it's suspended until re-certified.

What Jev is, and isn't

A decision model: text in, numbers out.

Text"Pull invoice fields from scanned PDFs for accounts payable"
→
Jev · one callTyped questions: yes/no, pick one, score 0–3
→
Numbersdocvision 0.91 · fit 2.8/3 · client data 0.07 (illustrative)
→
Plain codeCutoffs owned by risk turn numbers into a verdict, with a trace

It is

  • Fast and cheap enough to sit in every workflow: we show the measured latency and cost on screen
  • Prose-free, so there's no invented text to review
  • Testable: every decision is scored against labelled examples

It isn't

  • A chatbot or a code writer
  • The certifier: Pantheon and people are
  • Approved for real data yet: that needs vendor and model risk review

The demo

Follow one request through the marketplace.

  1. Discover. "Pull invoice fields from scanned PDFs": reuse Docvision, already certified.
  2. Compliance catch. "Translate client letters": the Translation Service fits, but it's certified only up to confidential data, so reuse needs a privacy review.
  3. Passport. Verify Docvision's certificate: signature, expiry, code unchanged.
  4. Tamper. Change "medium" risk to "low": the signature fails.
  5. Change. A new commit lands: the passport is suspended until re-certified.
